Day One for Jira

Day One makes sure new hires have what they need on their first day. An onboarding template lists the preparation tasks (laptop, accounts, desk, welcome email, training...), who owns each one (IT, Facilities, HR, the hiring manager or a buddy) and when it is due relative to the start date. Day One creates the work items in Jira, tells you for every new hire whether day one is ready, at risk or late, shows which team is holding things up, and reminds owners of overdue tasks.

Setup

  1. Install the app in Jira and open Apps → Day One → Settings as a Jira administrator.
  2. Enter the key of the project where onboarding work items are created. It needs a standard work type (for example Task) and a subtask type; a business project works out of the box.
  3. Choose the person who does each team's tasks (IT, Facilities, HR...). "manager" and "buddy" are taken from each onboarding.
  4. Adjust the template in Templates: tasks, owners, due day (0 = day one, -3 = three days before) and whether the task is needed on day one.
  5. In Start onboarding, enter the new hire, start date, hiring manager and optional buddy. Day One creates one work item for the new hire and one subtask per task.
  6. The Board shows every new hire's readiness; the Day One panel on the work item shows progress per phase and what is blocking day one.
  7. Rovo: choose the agent Onboarding Coordinator and ask "Who starts in the next 7 days, and are they ready?". The agent is read-only. The same tools are available through MCP.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Day One runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

  • What the app reads: the onboarding work items it created and their subtasks (summary, status, due date, assignee), with the permissions of the user viewing them; the daily reminder reads the same as the app.
  • What the app changes: when a user starts an onboarding, it creates one work item and its subtasks in the configured project and stores the plan (new hire name, start date, manager, buddy, template and task keys) as a property of that work item. The daily reminder adds one comment to an overdue task (once per due date). Nothing else is changed.
  • What the app stores: its settings (project key and the account ID of the person for each team), the templates and the list of reminders sent, in Forge storage on your site. The onboarding plan itself is a property of the onboarding work item in Jira.
  • Sharing: none. No analytics, no third-party services, no outbound network access, and no language model is called by the app itself.
  • AI: the Rovo agent and MCP tools return the same facts the board shows; the language model is Atlassian Rovo, operated by Atlassian under your Atlassian agreement.
