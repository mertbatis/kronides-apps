Triage Compass for Jira

Triage Compass helps software teams triage incoming bugs. For each new issue it finds similar earlier issues (an open duplicate, or a regression of something already fixed), works out which team owns this kind of problem from the Team field of similar issues and the Atlassian Teamwork Graph, and suggests who in that team should take it based on hands-on experience and current open work. A person confirms; the app sets Team and assignee, links the duplicate and leaves a comment that explains the decision. A Rovo agent (also available to external AI agents through MCP) answers "is this a duplicate?", "who should fix it?" and "what needs triage?".

Setup

  1. Install the app in Jira. Installing apps that read the Teamwork Graph requires an organization admin.
  2. Open any issue and expand the Triage Compass panel (or use the app button on the issue).
  3. Review the evidence, adjust team or assignee if needed, and press Apply and comment. Changes use your own Jira permissions.
  4. Open the project page Triage queue to see every open issue with no assignee and no team.
  5. Rovo: open Rovo chat and choose the agent Triage Compass. The agent is read-only.

Teams come from Atlassian Teams. For the best routing, set the Team field on issues as you resolve them; Triage Compass learns from it immediately, no training step.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Triage Compass runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

  • What the app reads: issue summaries, descriptions, statuses, assignees and Team field values in the project of the issue being triaged; from the Teamwork Graph: the teams people belong to, team members, and pull requests, deployments and goals linked to issues. All reads use the permissions of the user who is viewing.
  • What the app changes: only what a user explicitly confirms: the Team field, the assignee, the label "triaged", one issue link and one comment on the triaged issue.
  • What the app stores: a triage log (issue key, team and assignee names, linked issue, the display name and account ID of the user who confirmed it, and the time) in Forge storage inside your Atlassian site, limited to the latest 200 entries.
  • Sharing: none. No analytics, no third-party services, no outbound network access. Similarity is computed inside the app; no issue text is sent to any language model by the app itself.
  • AI: the Rovo agent and MCP tools are read-only and return the same facts the panel shows; the language model is Atlassian Rovo, operated by Atlassian under your Atlassian agreement.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app deletes its stored data according to Atlassian Forge's data lifecycle.
