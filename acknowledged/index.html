Acknowledged for Confluence

Acknowledged collects read-and-understood sign-off for Confluence pages such as policies and procedures. A page owner chooses the audience (Teamwork Graph teams and named people), readers confirm the exact version they read, overdue people are reminded with an @-mention comment, and the owner can export the evidence as CSV.

Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests: open an issue at github.com/mertbatis/kronides-apps/issues. We aim to answer within two business days.

Setup

  1. Install the app in Confluence.
  2. On a page you can edit, click Acknowledged under the title, choose teams and people, a deadline and whether to renew (for example every 12 months), and press Ask for acknowledgement.
  3. Readers see the request under the title and press I have read and understood version N.
  4. Each space has an Acknowledgements page (Space apps); everyone has My acknowledgements (Apps).

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Acknowledged runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

  • What the app reads: the page you open (title, version, content to compute a fingerprint), its version authors, people's display names, and team membership from the Teamwork Graph, with the permissions of the user who is asking.
  • What the app stores (Forge storage, in your Atlassian site's region): for each page that asks for acknowledgement, the required version and a SHA-256 fingerprint of its content, the audience (team names and Atlassian account IDs with display names), and each acknowledgement (account ID, version, fingerprint, time). Page content itself is not stored. Data is removed when the owner presses Stop asking or the app is uninstalled.
  • What the app changes: it adds a footer comment that @-mentions overdue people when an owner asks for a reminder, and at most once a week per person from a daily job. It never edits pages.
  • Sharing: none. No analytics, no third-party services, no outbound network access, no language model.
