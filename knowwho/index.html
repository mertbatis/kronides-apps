Know Who for Jira

Know Who answers "who knows about this?". For a topic, it looks at who resolved, worked on and commented on related work items in Jira and who wrote or updated related pages in Confluence, ranks people by recent hands-on work, and adds their teams from the Atlassian Teamwork Graph. It is available as a global page, as a panel on every work item ("Who knows about this?"), and as a Rovo agent and MCP tool.

Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests: open an issue at github.com/mertbatis/kronides-apps/issues. We aim to answer within two business days.

Setup

  1. Install the app in Jira, and in Confluence on the same site to include pages.
  2. Open Apps → Know Who, type a topic and press Find people, or open the Who knows about this? panel on a work item.
  3. Rovo: choose the agent Know Who and ask "Who knows about the payment gateway?". The agent is read-only.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Know Who runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

  • What the app reads: work items and pages that match the topic (titles, assignee, reporter, comment authors and dates; page authors, editors and dates) and team membership from the Teamwork Graph, always with the permissions of the user who is asking: people only see evidence they are allowed to see.
  • What the app changes: nothing. Know Who is read-only.
  • What the app stores: nothing. Results are computed on request and not saved.
  • Sharing: none. No analytics, no third-party services, no outbound network access, and no language model is called by the app itself.
  • AI: the Rovo agent and MCP tool return the same facts the page shows; the language model is Atlassian Rovo, operated by Atlassian under your Atlassian agreement.
  • Ranking: only by work evidence (role and recency), never by personal attributes.
