SoD Guard for Jira

SoD Guard checks segregation of duties in Jira change management: the person who implements a change must not be the person who approves it. It shows the chain of custody on every issue, a compliance dashboard with evidence export, an optional workflow validator that blocks self-approval, and a Rovo agent (also available to external AI agents through MCP) that answers audit questions.

Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests: open an issue at github.com/mertbatis/kronides-apps/issues. We aim to answer within two business days.

Setup

  1. Install the app in Jira. Detection works immediately with no configuration: statuses in the "In Progress" category count as build statuses, statuses in the "Done" category count as approval.
  2. Open Apps → SoD Guard for the dashboard. Jira administrators can adjust the policy on the Policy tab.
  3. Optional blocking: edit a workflow, select the transition into your approval status, add the rule SoD Guard: block self-approval and publish.
  4. Rovo: open Rovo chat and choose the agent SoD Auditor.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

SoD Guard runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

  • What the app reads: issue keys, summaries, types, statuses, assignees, reporters and the status/assignee change history of issues, using the permissions of the user who is viewing (or of the app for the workflow validator and the daily scan).
  • What the app stores: the policy you configure and a daily count of approved issues and violations, in Forge storage inside your Atlassian site. It does not store issue content or personal profiles. Atlassian account IDs are only held in memory while a request is processed.
  • Sharing: none. No analytics, no third-party services, no outbound network access.
  • AI: the Rovo agent and MCP tools return the same facts the dashboard shows; the language model is Atlassian Rovo, operated by Atlassian under your Atlassian agreement.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app deletes its stored data according to Atlassian Forge's data lifecycle.
